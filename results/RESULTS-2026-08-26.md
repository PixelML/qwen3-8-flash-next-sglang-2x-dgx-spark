## Before/after interpretation

The model has 176B total parameters and 6B active parameters per token. Active
parameters are a compute estimate, not a latency guarantee. Single-stream
decode also pays for recurrent/QSA kernels, expert routing, weight movement,
and cross-node synchronization at every tensor-parallel layer.

The verified NEXTN/MTP profile improves repeatable single-stream throughput by
82% (26.09 to 47.54 tok/s) and concurrency-16 aggregate throughput by 9%
(251.83 to 275.37 tok/s). Concurrency 4 was 2.6% lower, while concurrency 8 was
3.0% higher. The main benefit is low-latency coding use: accepted draft tokens
amortize recurrent/QSA kernel, expert-routing, memory, and cross-node costs.

MTP adds roughly 1.45–1.49 GB of draft weights per rank and reserves more
Mamba state. That is why the effective request limit falls from the configured
36 to 25 while the 262,144-token context remains available.
